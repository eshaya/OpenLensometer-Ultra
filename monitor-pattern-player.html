Lensometer: setup and procedure

The phone measures a lens by looking through it at patterns on this screen. Read this once, then press Return to go to the alignment page.

Notice: OpenLensometer is experimental software for educational and research use. It is not a medical device and has not been cleared or approved by the FDA or any other regulator. Its readings may be wrong and are not a prescription. It is no substitute for an eye examination or a professional lensmeter. Do not use it to order glasses or to make decisions about eye health. Provided "as is", without warranty of any kind.

You need

  1. The phone with the OpenLensometer app.
  2. A tripod or stand that holds the phone still for the whole session.
  3. The glasses, and a ruler.

Setup

  1. Show this page on the monitor, with the brightness up and no lamp or window reflecting in the screen.
  2. Put the phone on the tripod with its back camera facing this browser window, square to the screen, about 50 cm away and level with the middle of the screen. Leave it there until you are done: the phone must not move between steps.
  3. Start the app on the phone.
  4. On the next page, set Monitor diagonal so the outer black ring of the bullseye measures 50 mm with the ruler. The phone judges its distance from that size. The setting is remembered.

Procedure

  1. Align. On the next page, go fullscreen first: press F or click Present fullscreen. Move and turn the tripod until the pink box on the phone sits on the phone's box. Keep both tilts shown at the top of the phone screen (Tilt L/R and Tilt F/B) within 6° of zero and the distance at 46–54 cm; the box turns green when the alignment is acceptable. Keep the glasses out of view and press Return once: a grid is shown and the phone records it as the reference.
  2. Lens size. Press L. On the white screen, hold the glasses flat against the monitor with the lens to measure centered on the plus sign, for a few seconds.
  3. Measure. The grid comes back by itself. Hold the glasses in front of the camera, about 20 cm from it, arms toward the phone. Place the lens so it is inside the outline the phone draws on its screen (it does not have to fill it), and hold still until the phone beeps. The phone then shows sphere, cylinder and axis, and the add for bifocals and progressives.
  4. Other lens. Press L again and repeat steps 2 and 3.
  5. Again. G measures the same lens again, using the lens shape already taken (for example if time ran out); L retakes the shape as well. The Reset capture button on the phone restarts the whole sequence from the alignment.
